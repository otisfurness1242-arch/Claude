# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: November 19, 2026**

Charlie's Lamps ("we," "us," or "our") operates **charlieslamps.store** (the "Site"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share your personal information when you visit the Site or buy from us.

## 1. Information We Collect

**Information you give us:**
- Name, email address, phone number, and billing and shipping addresses
- Order details, such as products purchased and order history
- Messages you send us, such as emails and support requests

**Payment information:** Payments are processed by our payment providers (such as Shopify Payments, PayPal, and others shown at checkout). We do not store your full card number.

**Information collected automatically:**
- IP address, browser type, device type, and operating system
- Pages viewed, time spent on the Site, and how you arrived at the Site
- Information collected through cookies, pixels, and similar technologies (see Section 5)

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

- Process, produce, ship, and deliver your order
- Send order confirmations, tracking updates, and customer service messages
- Detect and prevent fraud, and respond to payment disputes and chargebacks
- Keep records of your acceptance of our Terms of Service and policies
- Improve the Site and our products
- Send marketing emails or texts if you have agreed to receive them (you can unsubscribe at any time)
- Comply with legal obligations

## 3. How We Share Your Information

**We do not sell your personal information for money.** We share information only with:

- **Shopify**, our e-commerce platform, which hosts our store and processes orders
- **Payment processors**, to process payments and prevent fraud
- **Production and fulfillment partners and shipping carriers**, to produce and deliver your order (they receive only what they need, such as your name and shipping address)
- **Marketing and analytics providers** (such as Google and Meta, the owner of Facebook and Instagram), to understand how the Site is used and show relevant ads
- **Banks, card issuers, and payment providers**, when needed to respond to a payment dispute or chargeback
- **Law enforcement or authorities**, when required by law or to protect our rights

## 4. Payment Disputes

If you file a chargeback or payment dispute, we may share your order records, IP address, checkout agreement, tracking and delivery information, and communications with you with the relevant bank, card issuer, or payment provider to respond to the dispute.

## 5. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We and our partners use cookies, pixels, and similar technologies to keep the Site working (for example, your cart), remember your preferences, analyze traffic, and show advertising. You can control cookies through your browser settings. Turning off some cookies may affect how the Site works. Where required by law, we will ask for your consent before using non-essential cookies.

For more on how Shopify uses your data, see https://www.shopify.com/legal/privacy.

## 6. Data Retention

We keep order information for as long as needed to fulfill orders, respond to disputes and chargebacks, and meet tax, accounting, and legal requirements, generally at least 7 years. We delete or anonymize information we no longer need.

## 7. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

- Access the personal information we hold about you
- Correct inaccurate information
- Request deletion of your information (subject to legal exceptions, such as records we must keep for tax purposes or active disputes)
- Opt out of marketing communications
- Opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising, where applicable (for example, under California law)
- Object to or restrict certain processing, and request a copy of your data (for example, under EU/UK law)

To make a request, email **pzrezells@gmail.com**. We may need to verify your identity. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

**California residents:** In the past 12 months we have collected the categories of information listed in Section 1 for the purposes in Section 2, and shared them as described in Section 3. Information shared with advertising partners may be considered "sharing" under California law. You may opt out by contacting us.

**EU/UK residents:** We process your information to perform our contract with you (fulfilling your order), to comply with legal obligations, for our legitimate interests (such as fraud prevention and responding to disputes), and with your consent (such as marketing). You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

## 8. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your information, and our platform (Shopify) uses industry-standard security. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

## 9. Children

The Site is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

## 10. International Transfers

Your information may be stored and processed in countries other than your own, including the United States and the countries where our fulfillment partners are located.

## 11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

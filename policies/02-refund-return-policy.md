# Refund & Return Policy

**Last updated: November 19, 2026**

Please read this policy carefully before ordering. By checking the box at checkout and completing your purchase, you confirm that you have read and agree to this policy.

## Every Lamp Is Made to Order

Each Charlie's Lamps product is produced individually after you place your order. As soon as you order, we purchase the materials and begin producing your item. We do not keep pre-made stock, which means we cannot resell a returned item. **For this reason, all sales are final.**

## No Returns, Refunds, or Exchanges

**We do not accept returns or exchanges and we do not give refunds**, including for:

- Change of mind, or no longer wanting the item
- Ordering the wrong product, color, size, or quantity
- Not liking the product after it arrives
- Minor variations in color, size, finish, or brightness compared to product photos (normal for made-to-order products)
- Delivery times that are still within our stated delivery window of **25 to 45 business days**
- Packages marked as delivered by the carrier that are later lost or stolen (unless you purchased Shipping Protection; see our Shipping Policy)
- Incorrect or incomplete shipping addresses entered by you at checkout

The only exceptions are described below.

## Order Cancellations: 48-Hour Window

You may cancel your order for a **full refund within 48 hours (2 days) of placing it.** To cancel, email **pzrezells@gmail.com** with the subject line **"Cancel Order"** and include your order number.

After 48 hours, your materials have been purchased and production has started, and **your order can no longer be cancelled or refunded.** Cancellation requests are timed by when we receive your email.

## Damaged Items

If your lamp arrives damaged, we're here to help. To be eligible:

1. **Contact us within 48 hours of delivery** (based on the carrier's delivery date) at **pzrezells@gmail.com** with the subject line **"Damaged Item"** and your order number.
2. Include **clear photos or a video** showing the damage and the shipping packaging.
3. Keep the item and all packaging until your claim is resolved. **Do not send the item back unless we ask you to.**

If your claim is approved, we will offer **a partial refund of up to 50% of the item price**, depending on how severe the damage is. We decide the amount after reviewing the photos or video you send. If you purchased Shipping Protection, see our Shipping Policy for how damaged items are handled.

Claims sent after 48 hours, or without photo or video evidence, may not be eligible. Damage caused after delivery, by misuse, by an incorrect power source, or by normal wear and tear is not covered.

## Wrong Item Received

If you receive a different product than the one you ordered, contact us within **48 hours of delivery** with photos of the item and its packaging. If we confirm the error was ours, we will send you the correct item at no cost.

## Orders Not Received

If your order has **not arrived within 45 business days** of purchase, contact us at **pzrezells@gmail.com** with your order number. We will investigate with the carrier, and if your package is confirmed lost in transit before delivery, we will send a replacement. Please do not file a dispute with your bank before contacting us. See Section 9 of our Terms of Service.

## Delays Beyond Our Delivery Window

If we learn that we will not be able to ship your order within our stated timeframe, we will notify you by email with a revised shipping date. You may then either agree to the new date or cancel your order for a full refund of the unshipped item.

## How Approved Refunds Are Issued

Approved refunds (cancellations and partial refunds) are issued to your **original payment method** within **5 to 10 business days** of approval. Your bank or card issuer may take additional time to post the refund to your account.

## Shipping Protection Fees

Shipping Protection fees are non-refundable once your order has shipped.

Nothing in this policy limits any rights you may have under the consumer protection laws of your country or state that cannot be waived by agreement.

# Chargeback Evidence Checklist (internal, not for publishing)

Use this when responding to a dispute in Shopify Payments or PayPal. Respond before the deadline and keep your response short, factual, and organized.

## Always include

- [ ] Order details: order number, date and time, amount, product
- [ ] Customer's IP address, and that it matches their billing/shipping region
- [ ] Proof the customer checked the agreement box at checkout (screenshot of the checkout with the checkbox and its wording)
- [ ] Screenshots of your Terms of Service, Refund Policy, and Shipping Policy **as they appeared on the date of the order**
- [ ] Screenshot of the product page showing the "made to order / 25–45 business days / all sales final" notice
- [ ] Order confirmation email sent to the customer (shows they were told delivery times, tracking, and the 48-hour cancellation window)
- [ ] All emails or messages with the customer
- [ ] Tracking information, **ideally the carrier tracking showing "delivered"** to the address on the order

## By dispute reason

**"Item not received"**
- Carrier tracking showing delivery to the customer's address (this is the most important piece; without it, this dispute is very hard to win)
- If the dispute was filed before 45 business days had passed: point out the stated delivery window the customer agreed to
- Note whether the customer bought Shipping Protection
- If the order truly never arrived after 45 business days, refund it in full (per your policy) instead of fighting the dispute

**Damaged item claims**
- Whether the customer sent photos of damaged packaging; your policy only covers items that arrive in visibly damaged packaging
- Your reply and any 50% refund already issued

**"Not as described / defective"**
- Product page photos and description
- Policy wording on normal made-to-order variations and the AI-image disclosure (product page notice, checkbox, Refund Policy "Product Images" section)
- Any photos the customer sent, and your response (e.g. the partial refund offered)

**"Cancelled / credit not processed"**
- Timestamp of the order vs. the timestamp of the cancellation request (shows it was after the 48-hour window)
- Refund Policy cancellation section

**"Fraudulent / unauthorized"**
- IP address, billing/shipping address match, AVS/CVV results from Shopify
- Customer's previous orders, if any
- Any email from the customer acknowledging the order

## Tips

- Save a dated screenshot (or PDF) of every policy page each time you edit it.
- Reply to every customer email within 1–2 business days, so the record shows you tried to help.
- Disputes filed while you were still resolving the issue are usually won by showing the open conversation.

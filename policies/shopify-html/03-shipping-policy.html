Delivery Time: 25 to 45 Business Days

Every lamp is made to order, so delivery takes longer than ordinary retail. Please allow 25 to 45 business days from the date of your order to receive it. This includes:

  • Production: we purchase materials and produce your lamp after you order.
  • Shipping: your lamp is handed to the carrier and delivered to you.

Business days are Monday through Friday, excluding public holidays. Weekends and holidays are not counted. For example, 45 business days is roughly 9 weeks.

These are estimates, not guarantees. Delivery may take longer during holidays and peak seasons, or because of carrier delays, customs inspections, weather, or other events beyond our control.

An order is not considered late or “not received” until 45 business days have passed since the purchase date.

Order Tracking: How It Works

Please read this so you know what to expect:

  1. When your order is confirmed: you will receive an email with your tracking number. Tracking will show that your order has been confirmed and that production has started.
  2. During production: the next tracking update can take around 2 weeks, and sometimes longer during busy periods with high order volume or a production backlog. Your lamp is being made during this time. A pause in tracking updates during production is normal and does not mean your order is lost or delayed.
  3. Once production is finished: your lamp is handed to the carrier and tracking will update as it moves toward you. You can follow it using the same tracking number and on your order status page.

If your tracking has not updated for more than 20 business days, please email us at pzrezells@gmail.com with your order number and we will look into it.

Delivered, Lost, or Stolen Packages

Once the carrier marks your package as delivered to the address you provided, responsibility for the package passes to you. We are not responsible for packages that are lost, stolen, or misplaced after delivery. If tracking shows delivered but you can’t find your package, we recommend:

  • Checking around your property, mailbox, and with neighbors or building management.
  • Waiting 1 to 2 business days, as carriers sometimes mark packages delivered early.
  • Contacting your local carrier office.

Unless you purchased Shipping Protection, packages marked as delivered are not eligible for a refund or replacement.

Shipping Protection

Shipping Protection is an optional add-on offered at checkout. It is not added automatically. You must choose to add it yourself, and its price is shown before you pay.

If you purchased Shipping Protection and your package is:

  • Stolen or missing after being marked delivered, or
  • Delivered in visibly damaged packaging,

contact us within 7 days of the delivery date at pzrezells@gmail.com with the subject line “Shipping Protection Claim”, your order number, and, for damaged packaging, photos or video of the packaging as it arrived. Once your claim is approved, we will send you a free replacement.

Orders that never arrive within 45 business days are refunded in full for all customers, with or without Shipping Protection. See our Refund & Return Policy.

Shipping Protection fees are non-refundable once an order has shipped. Shipping Protection does not cover incorrect addresses entered by the customer, or packages refused or not collected.

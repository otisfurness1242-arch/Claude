Every Lamp Is Made to Order

Each Charlie’s Lamps product is produced individually after you place your order. As soon as you order, we purchase the materials and begin producing your item. We do not keep pre-made stock, which means we cannot resell a returned item. For this reason, we do not offer refunds, returns, or exchanges for change of mind.

No Refunds for Change of Mind

We do not accept returns or exchanges and we do not give refunds for change of mind, including:

  • No longer wanting the item

  • Not liking the look of the product after it arrives
  • Minor variations in color, size, finish, or brightness compared to product photos (normal for made-to-order products)
  • Slight differences between the real product and our AI-generated or AI-edited product images and marketing (see “Product Images” below)
  • Delivery times that are still within our stated delivery window of 25 to 45 business days
  • Incorrect or incomplete shipping addresses entered by you at checkout

This does not apply to products that are faulty, damaged, or not as described. See “Damaged or Faulty Items” below.

Order Cancellations: 48-Hour Window

You may cancel your order for a full refund within 48 hours (2 days) of placing it. To cancel, email pzrezells@gmail.com with the subject line “Cancel Order” and include your order number.

After 48 hours, your materials have been purchased and production has started, and your order can no longer be cancelled for change of mind. Cancellation requests are timed by when we receive your email.

Damaged or Faulty Items

Every lamp is checked and securely packed before it ships. If there is a problem with your order when it arrives, please contact us within 48 hours of delivery so we can sort it out quickly. Email pzrezells@gmail.com with the subject line “Damaged Item”, your order number, and:

  • Clear photos or a video of the shipping packaging as it arrived, before or while opening it, and
  • Photos or a video of the product showing the problem.

Keep the item and all packaging until your claim is resolved. Do not send the item back unless we ask you to.

Damaged packaging on arrival: if the outer shipping packaging arrived visibly damaged (for example crushed, torn, punctured, or water-damaged), we will offer you a 50% refund of the item price, and you keep the lamp.

Other faults: if the product is faulty or not as described in any other way, we will assess your claim and provide a remedy as required by the Consumer Guarantees Act, which may be a repair, replacement, or refund depending on the problem.

Damage caused after delivery, by misuse, by an incorrect power source, or by normal wear and tear is not covered. If you purchased Shipping Protection, see our Shipping Policy for how damaged packages are handled.

If we learn that we will not be able to ship your order within our stated timeframe, we will notify you by email with a revised shipping date. You may then either agree to the new date or cancel your order for a full refund.

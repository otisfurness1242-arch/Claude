# Shipping Policy

**Last updated: November 19, 2026**

By completing your purchase, you confirm that you have read and agree to this Shipping Policy, including our delivery times.

## Delivery Time: 25 to 45 Business Days

**Every lamp is made to order, so delivery takes longer than ordinary retail.** Please allow **25 to 45 business days** from the date of your order to receive it. This includes:

- **Production:** we purchase materials and produce your lamp after you order.
- **Shipping:** your lamp is handed to the carrier and delivered to you.

Business days are Monday through Friday, excluding public holidays. Weekends and holidays are not counted. For example, 45 business days is roughly **9 weeks**.

These are estimates, not guarantees. Delivery may take longer during holidays and peak seasons, or because of carrier delays, customs inspections, weather, or other events beyond our control.

**An order is not considered late or "not received" until 45 business days have passed since the purchase date.**

## Order Tracking: How It Works

Please read this so you know what to expect:

1. **At purchase:** you will receive an order confirmation email with an **initial tracking number.**
2. **During production (up to about 30 business days):** **the initial tracking number will not show any updates or movement.** This is normal. Your lamp is still being made and has not been handed to a carrier yet.
3. **Once shipped (around 30 business days after purchase):** your order is handed to the shipping carrier, and you will receive tracking that shows live updates. **This carrier tracking number may be different from your initial number.** We will email it to you and it will appear on your order status page.

If your tracking still shows no updates after **35 business days**, please email us at **pzrezells@gmail.com** with your order number and we will look into it.

## Shipping Address

You are responsible for entering a complete and correct shipping address at checkout. Please double-check it before placing your order. If you made a mistake, email us **within 48 hours** of ordering so we can try to correct it. After that, we may not be able to change it. We are not responsible for orders shipped to an incorrect address that you provided, and those orders are not eligible for a refund or replacement.

## Delivered, Lost, or Stolen Packages

**Once the carrier marks your package as delivered to the address you provided, responsibility for the package passes to you.** We are not responsible for packages that are lost, stolen, or misplaced after delivery. If tracking shows delivered but you can't find your package, we recommend:

- Checking around your property, mailbox, and with neighbors or building management.
- Waiting 1 to 2 business days, as carriers sometimes mark packages delivered early.
- Contacting your local carrier office.

**Unless you purchased Shipping Protection, packages marked as delivered are not eligible for a refund or replacement.**

## Shipping Protection

Shipping Protection is an optional add-on offered at checkout. Its price is shown clearly in your cart before payment, and **you can remove it before completing your order.**

If you purchased Shipping Protection and your package is:

- **Lost in transit** (tracking stops updating and the package never arrives),
- **Stolen** after being marked delivered, or
- **Damaged** in transit,

contact us within **7 days** of the delivery date (or, for lost packages, after 45 business days from purchase) at **pzrezells@gmail.com** with your order number and, for damage, photos or video. Once your claim is approved, we will send you **a free replacement.**
